Important limitations

This tool provides an illustrative estimate, not tax, payroll, legal or immigration advice. UK tax is estimated using 2026/27 England income-tax bands for both assignment years. Swiss tax and employee social-security deductions are entered by the user. The model does not automatically determine the applicable social-security system, validate a certificate of coverage, or include every possible payroll adjustment or employer charge. Confirm the facts and applicable treatment before relying on the figures.

5. Immigration guidance

Work permission for the two-year assignment

The likely route to assess is the Senior or Specialist Worker visa under the Global Business Mobility route. It may allow an eligible employee to work at their employer's UK branch. Eligibility must be confirmed before relying on this route.

The employer should:

  • Confirm that the UK entity is an approved sponsor, or apply for a sponsor licence.
  • Check the eligible occupation code, going rate and applicable salary requirement.
  • Assign a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) describing the UK role.
  • Confirm that the employee meets the route's eligibility and overseas-employment requirements.
  • Coordinate the application before the employee starts the UK assignment.

The published salary threshold is £52,500 or the applicable going rate for the occupation, whichever is higher. The occupation code and qualifying salary need to be checked for the specific role.

Official sources: Visa overview | Eligibility and salary rules | Sponsor licence

Indicative visa costs and processing time

  • Published application fee for an application from outside the UK for a visa of up to three years: £819 per applicant.
  • The Immigration Health Surcharge is usually £1,035 per year for an adult applicant; the actual amount depends on the visa granted.
  • The Certificate of Sponsorship fee is listed as £525 and is normally paid by the sponsoring employer.
  • An Immigration Skills Charge may also apply to the employer, subject to the sponsor's circumstances and any exemption.
  • Standard processing from outside the UK is usually around three weeks after the required identity checks and application steps, but can vary.

These are indicative published charges and timings, not a complete employer budget. Check the official pages before applying.

Official sources: Visa fees | Healthcare surcharge | Immigration Skills Charge | Application and processing

Business visit before the assignment

A Swiss citizen visiting briefly for permitted business activities may need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), unless an exemption applies. Permitted activities can include business meetings and conferences. An ETA does not authorise ordinary work for a UK company. Check the precise planned activities against the Visitor Rules.

The published ETA fee is £20. Each traveller who needs an ETA must obtain their own authorisation before travelling.

Official sources: Visiting as a Swiss citizen | ETA requirements and fee | What an ETA permits

Passport requirements

The traveller should check the current UK entry requirements and hold the required valid passport and travel authorisation. An ETA is not a substitute for the work visa needed for a long-term assignment.

Official source: UK entry requirements for Swiss citizens

Social security is a separate decision

UK–Swiss social-security coordination rules may affect whether UK National Insurance applies. The answer depends on the employee's circumstances and whether the appropriate certificate of coverage can be obtained. A certificate is not immigration permission and does not remove the need to establish the correct visa route.

Official source: UK guidance for workers arriving from Switzerland
